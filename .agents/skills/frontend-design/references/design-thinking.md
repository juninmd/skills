# Design Thinking and Aesthetic Directions

Methodology for choosing and committing to a bold visual direction.

## 1. Context Assessment
- **Purpose:** What problem is being solved? Who is the audience?
- **Constraints:** Framework, performance, accessibility.
- **Differentiation:** What makes this unforgettable?

## 2. Aesthetic Flavors
Commit to a clear conceptual direction such as:
- **Minimal:** Brutalist/raw, industrial, utility-first, luxury/refined.
- **Expressive:** Maximalist chaos, retro-futuristic, organic/natural.
- **Thematic:** Art deco, editorial/magazine, playful/toy-like.

## 3. Intentionality
Choose one direction and execute with precision. Elegance comes from executing the vision well, whether through bold maximalism or refined minimalism.
