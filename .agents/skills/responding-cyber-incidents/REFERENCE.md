## Tools
- **SIEM:** Splunk, ELK Stack (Elasticsearch, Logstash, Kibana)
- **EDR:** CrowdStrike, SentinelOne, Carbon Black
- **Forensics:** Autopsy, Volatility, Wireshark
- **Analysis:** Cuckoo Sandbox, ANY.RUN

## Common Incident Types
- **Phishing:** Deceptive emails/messages.
- **Malware:** Viruses, worms, trojans, ransomware.
- **DDoS:** Distributed Denial of Service.
- **Ransomware:** Encrypting files for ransom.
- **Insider Threat:** Malicious or negligent employees.
- **Advanced Persistent Threat (APT):** Sophisticated, long-term attacks.
