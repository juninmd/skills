# Applying KISS

## Concept
Simple solutions are easier to maintain, test, and evolve.

## Guidelines
1. Avoid over-engineering for hypothetical scenarios.
2. Prefer straightforward logic over "clever" constructs that are hard to maintain.
3. Use known patterns before creating new abstractions.
4. Ensure readability for less experienced devs.

## Execution
- Replace complex blocks with simpler equivalent approaches.
- Reduce nesting with guard clauses.
- Remove unused parameters and flexibility.
